New pages, native network and Bluetooth settings, search, and a pacman package.

### Network
- **Ethernet:** status, link speed, address, gateway and DNS, plain-language diagnosis (no cable, 10 Mb/s link, no DHCP address), connect/disconnect, IPv4 automatic or manual, custom DNS, auto-connect.
- **Wi-Fi:** radio on/off, nearby networks with signal and security, connect (hidden networks too), saved networks with IPv4/DNS settings and forget. Passwords go through libnm and never appear on a command line.

### Bluetooth
- On/off, paired devices with battery level, connect/disconnect, forget.
- Search and pair, with pairing codes shown or confirmed in a dialog.

### Power (new page)
- Power profile.
- Night light: on/off, warmth and a daily schedule.
- What the power button does.
- Screensaver and lock times (moved from the Bar page).

### System (new page)
- Time zone (searchable) and automatic time.
- System language.
- Default browser, terminal and editor.
- Notifications: do not disturb and recent notifications.
- Printers (CUPS web interface).
- About this computer, with "Update Omarchy".

### Sound (new page)
- Output and input device, with volume and mute; optional volume above 100%; a test sound.
- Volume and mute for each app playing or using the microphone.
- Updates live.

### Search
- **Ctrl+F** finds any setting by name on every page, ignoring accents and case, and jumps to it.

### Also
- **☰ → Restore app defaults**, with a dated backup and Undo.
- Screenshots in the README.

### Packaging
- `packaging/arch/PKGBUILD`, also attached here: `makepkg -si` installs the app system-wide.
