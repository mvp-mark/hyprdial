A new Apps page, Omarchy updates and snapshots in About, and the MIT license.

### Apps (new page)
- **Default apps:** browser, terminal and editor move here from the System page, joined by the file manager, PDF documents and images (PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF). Only installed apps that open the type are listed.
- **Web apps:** the ones made with Omarchy, with their icon and address; add one (the site's own icon by default) or remove it.
- **Startup apps:** switch XDG autostart entries on or off for your user, add your own commands, and see the ones in `autostart.lua`.

### Sound
- Changing the output or input device also moves the apps playing or recording to it, through Omarchy's own commands.

### System
- About says whether an Omarchy update is available and highlights the Update button when there is one.
- "Create a snapshot now" makes a restore point in a terminal; restoring is done from the boot menu.

### Also
- The project is now under the MIT license.
- `packaging/arch/PKGBUILD`, also attached here: `makepkg -si` installs the app system-wide.
